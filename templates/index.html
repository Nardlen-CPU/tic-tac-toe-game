About

Arena Edition

Professional edition by Leon Mureti, 2026. This interface keeps classic tic tac toe fast while adding board scaling, seeded AI, league progression, Super Undefeated clocks, persistent trophies, pause/resume, save slots, keyboard play, motion feedback, and a tournament-style HUD.

Keyboard: use Tab to reach the board, arrow keys to move between available cells, and Enter or Space to play. P pauses, U undoes, R resets, and Ctrl+S saves. Escape closes dialogs.
